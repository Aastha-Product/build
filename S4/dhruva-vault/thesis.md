# Dhruva thesis: the six criteria

Dhruva Capital, Fund II (Rs 120 crore). Every application is checked against these six. For each, the verdict is match, partial, no-match, or insufficient-data, and every verdict needs a citation from the deck.

| Criterion | What a match looks like |
|---|---|
| stage | Pre-seed or seed |
| sector | Back-office and process software for businesses |
| geography | India-based |
| founder_profile | Founders with direct experience of the problem |
| business_model | Recurring or pilot revenue |
| round_size | Within the cheque range: Rs 50 lakh to Rs 2.5 crore |

See also [[anti-thesis]] and [[ira-rules]].
